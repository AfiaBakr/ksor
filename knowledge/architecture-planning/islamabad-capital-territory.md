## Authority

**Capital Development Authority (CDA)** — jurisdiction: Islamabad Capital
Territory (ICT).

- Official site: <https://www.cda.gov.pk/>[^cda-official]
- Legal basis: **Capital Development Authority Ordinance, 1960**[^cda-ordinance-1960]

CDA is a federal public-benefit corporation responsible for estate management,
project execution and sector development across ICT, and is the body that
approves building plans within Islamabad.

## Building regulations

Research for this record found references to the **Capital Development
Authority Building Control Regulations, 2020** as the applicable building
regulation in ICT, but the source located is a secondary summary rather than
the regulation text on cda.gov.pk itself.[^cda-building-regs-2020]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Open question: this record has not confirmed the 2020 regulation text
> directly from CDA's own site. Before relying on a specific clause (setback,
> height, ground-coverage or parking rule), obtain the regulation document
> directly from cda.gov.pk.

## Related municipal body

The **Islamabad Metropolitan Corporation (IMC)** also plays a municipal role
in Islamabad; this record has not established the boundary between IMC's
functions and CDA's building-control function, and treats CDA as the building
approval authority per the sources above.[^imc-wiki]
