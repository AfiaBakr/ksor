## Authority

**Peshawar Development Authority (PDA)** — jurisdiction: Peshawar.

- Official site found at two domains in this research: <https://www.pda.gkp.pk/>
  and <https://www.pda.kp.gov.pk/>.[^pda-official][^pda-official-alt]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Not verified: this record found two different official-looking PDA domains
> and has not established which is current. Confirm before publishing a
> resource link.

- Building by-laws are published for download by PDA.[^pda-bylaws]

## Provincial framework

- **The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Local Government Act, 2013** — the district
  council may approve bye-laws for functions devolved to other authorities,
  organisations or individuals.[^kp-lga-2013]
- **The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Model Building Bye-Laws, 2017** — the province-wide
  model building bye-law framework that authorities across KP apply.[^kp-model-bylaws-2017]
- **The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Land Use and Building Control (Amendment) Act,
  2024** — a recent amendment to the land-use and building-control legal
  framework.[^kp-land-use-amendment-2024]

## Other KP development authorities

KP has development authorities beyond Peshawar with their own building-control
rules — for example the **Galiyat Development Authority (Building Control)
Rules, 2012** for the Galiyat hill-station area.[^galiyat-rules-2012] This
record does not enumerate every KP city authority; treat any city not listed
here as an open question requiring its own lookup.
