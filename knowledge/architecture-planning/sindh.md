## Authority

**Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA)** — jurisdiction: the **entire
province of Sindh** (not Karachi alone).

- Official site: <https://www.sbca.gos.pk/>[^sbca-official]
- Legal basis: **The Sindh Building Control Ordinance, 1979**[^sbca-ordinance-1979]

SBCA began as the Karachi Building Control Authority (KBCA); the Government of
Sindh renamed it SBCA in 2011 and extended its jurisdiction province-wide. It
approves building plans and NOCs against building and town-planning
regulations, and issues licenses to professionals, builders and developers
under the **Karachi Building & Town Planning Regulations, 2002**.[^sbca-history]

## Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC)

KMC holds a separate municipal role in Karachi alongside SBCA's building-control
function; this record has not established the precise division of
responsibility between the two and treats SBCA as the building-approval
authority per the sources above.

## Hyderabad — Hyderabad Development Authority (HDA)

- Established 1976 under a Sindh act; revived/amended by the **Hyderabad
  Development Authority (Revival and Amending) Act, 2013**.[^hda-establishment]
