## Authority

**Quetta Development Authority (QDA)** — jurisdiction: Quetta.

- Official site: <https://qda.gob.pk/>[^qda-official]
- An agency of the Government of Balochistan, formed in 1978, responsible for
  municipal services in Quetta.[^qda-wiki]
- QDA exercises building control within its own housing and commercial
  schemes, and prepares, updates and implements the Quetta Master
  Plan.[^qda-about]

## Distinct from the provincial development body

The **Balochistan Development Authority** is a separate, province-level
entity from QDA (which is city-scoped to Quetta).[^bda-wiki] This record has
not established the Balochistan Development Authority's role in building
control specifically — treat as an open question.
