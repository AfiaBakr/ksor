## Authority

**Development Authority Muzaffarabad (DAM)** — jurisdiction: Muzaffarabad
city.

- Official site: <https://www.dam.gok.pk/>[^dam-official]
- Legal basis: **The Development Authority Muzaffarabad Act, 1989** (Act II of
  1989).[^dam-act-1989]
- DAM's objective is planned housing, master planning, and building control
  for Muzaffarabad. After the 2005 earthquake it was assigned reconstruction
  and rehabilitation of the city's civic area, and launched the Langarpura and
  Thotha satellite-town housing schemes to relocate master-plan-affected
  residents and people in hazard zones.[^dam-about]

## Territory-wide legal framework

**The Azad Jammu and Kashmir Building Control Ordinance, 2011** (AJK Ordinance
No. XXX of 2011) is the territory-wide legal basis: it allows the AJK
government to authorise bodies corporate, local councils, or other
organisations as the building-control **agency** for a specified
area.[^ajk-bco-2011]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Open question: this record has confirmed DAM as Muzaffarabad's designated
> authority, but has not confirmed which body is designated as the
> building-control agency for other AJK cities (Mirpur, Rawalakot, Kotli,
> etc.) under the 2011 Ordinance. Look up the specific designation for any
> site outside Muzaffarabad before relying on this record.
