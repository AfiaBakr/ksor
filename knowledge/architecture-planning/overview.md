There is no single building-control authority for Pakistan. Authority is
split three ways — by **territory** (federal capital, four provinces, two
separate territories), by **city** within a province (each major city usually
has its own development authority), and by **enclave** (cantonments and
Defence Housing Authority estates sit outside the civilian development
authority for that city, under a military-administered track). A construction
project's applicable regulator depends on exactly where the site sits, not
just which province it is in.

## The three tracks that run in parallel everywhere

1. **The land-use / building-control authority** for the specific city or
   territory — this is who approves the building plan, issues the completion
   / occupancy certificate, and enforces zoning and building bye-laws. This is
   the authority named in each of the area documents below.
2. **Two federal professional-registration bodies**, which apply nationwide
   regardless of which area authority has jurisdiction:
   - **Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC)** registers consulting engineers and
     constructor/operator firms, and only PEC-registered firms may legally
     offer engineering consultancy or construction services on regulated
     projects.[^pec-official]
   - **Pakistan Council of Architects and Town Planners (PCATP)**, established
     1983 and headquartered in Islamabad, is the federal regulatory authority
     for architects and town planners.[^pcatp-official]
3. **A national structural/safety code**, which every area authority's
   building bye-laws reference for design compliance: the **Building Code of
   Pakistan – Seismic Provisions 2007**, approved by the Cabinet on 9 August
   2007 and notified vide S.R.O. 971(I)/2008 dated 8 September 2008, developed
   by a task force drawing on NESPAK, the Ministry of Housing & Works, the
   International Code Council and the American Concrete Institute.[^bcp-seismic-2007]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A 2021 update — **Building Code of Pakistan (2021)** — exists as a PEC
> document, but this record has not verified whether it has been formally
> notified as the legally applicable code (replacing the 2007 seismic
> provisions) or remains a draft/reference document. Confirm its current
> status with PEC or the relevant area authority before relying on it for a
> compliance decision.[^bcp-2021-draft]

## The enclave track: cantonments and DHA

Independently of provincial and city authorities, **43 notified cantonments**
across Pakistan are administered by Cantonment Boards under the Military
Lands & Cantonments (ML&C) Department, Ministry of Defence, per the
**Cantonments Act 1924**. Defence Housing Authority (DHA) estates that sit
inside cantonment limits run a hybrid regime — DHA's own rules govern internal
estate management, while the Cantonments Act governs public administration and
building control.[^cantonments-act-overview] See
[Cantonments and Defence Housing Authorities](cantonments-and-dha.md).
