Punjab regulates building through a **separate development authority per
major city**, each operating under the province-wide framework below.

## Provincial framework (applies in every Punjab city)

- **The Punjab Development of Cities Act, 1976** — the founding act under
  which the city development authorities (LDA, RDA, FDA, MDA, GDA, etc.) were
  established.[^pdca-1976]
- **Punjab Land Use (Classification, Reclassification and Redevelopment)
  Rules, 2009** (amended) — governs permitted, permissible and prohibited land
  use against master plans and approved schemes, through District Planning
  and Design Committees.[^punjab-land-use-rules-2009]
- **The Punjab Private Housing Schemes and Land Sub-Division Rules, 2010** —
  governs private housing-scheme approval and land sub-division.[^punjab-housing-schemes-rules-2010]

## Lahore — Lahore Development Authority (LDA)

- Official site: <https://lda.gop.pk/>[^lda-official]
- Regulation: **LDA Building and Zoning Regulations, 2007**, formulated under
  the LDA Act, 1975, amended through 2019 (chapterwise consolidated version
  available on the LDA site).[^lda-regs-2019]

## Rawalpindi — Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA)

- Official site: <https://rda.gop.pk/>[^rda-official]
- Regulation: **RDA Building & Zoning Regulations 2020**, covering setbacks,
  building heights, ground-coverage ratios and storey limits by plot
  size.[^rda-regs-2020]

## Faisalabad — Faisalabad Development Authority (FDA)

- Official site: <https://www.fda.gov.pk/>[^fda-official]

## Multan — Multan Development Authority (MDA)

- Established 22 October 1976 under the Punjab Development of Cities
  Act.[^mda-establishment]
