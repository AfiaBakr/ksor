## Legal basis

**Cantonments Act, 1924**, administered by the **Military Lands & Cantonments
(ML&C) Department, Ministry of Defence**. Pakistan has **43 notified
cantonments**, each governed by a Cantonment Board — a statutory body of
elected and nominated members chaired by a Cantonment Executive Officer (CEO)
— with general executive authority over land classification, building
permissions, lease grants and enforcement.[^cantonment-wiki]

This track runs **independently of** the provincial/city development
authorities covered elsewhere in this section: a site inside cantonment limits
is not under LDA, SBCA, CDA, etc. — it is under the local Cantonment Board.

## The DHA network

**Defence Housing Authority (DHA)** operates as a separate legal entity per
city — DHA Karachi, DHA Lahore, DHA Islamabad-Rawalpindi, and others — each
publishing its own building bye-laws and running its own Town Planning &
Building Control function.[^dha-karachi-official][^dha-lahore-official][^dha-ir-bylaws]

- DHA Karachi: <https://www.dhakarachi.org/>[^dha-karachi-official]
- DHA Lahore: <https://dhalahore.org/>[^dha-lahore-official]
- DHA Islamabad & Rawalpindi bye-laws:
  <https://www.dhai-r.com.pk/static/DHA_byelaws/DHAbyelaws.pdf>[^dha-ir-bylaws]

### Where DHA sits inside cantonment limits

Where a DHA estate falls within a cantonment's boundary, a **hybrid regime**
applies: DHA's own rules govern internal estate management, while the
Cantonments Act governs matters of public administration and building
control. DHA's building-control directorate coordinates with the relevant
Cantonment Board on such cases.[^cantonment-wiki]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Which regime governs a specific DHA plot (DHA rules alone, or DHA plus
> Cantonment Board) depends on whether that plot sits inside notified
> cantonment limits — confirm this for the specific site rather than assuming
> either way.
