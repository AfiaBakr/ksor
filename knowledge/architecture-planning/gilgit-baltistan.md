## Authority

**Gilgit Development Authority (GDA-GB)** — jurisdiction: Gilgit city.

- Official site: <https://gilgitdevelopmentauthority.gov.pk/>[^gda-gb-official]
- Established 2013, mandated to improve living conditions in Gilgit city
  through urban planning, infrastructural development, and zoning and
  land-use management.[^gda-gb-official]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Open question: this record did not locate a published building bye-law or
> building-control regulation document for GDA-GB in this research pass — only
> its general mandate. Confirm the applicable building bye-law directly with
> the authority before relying on it.

## Territorial status

Gilgit-Baltistan is a separate constitutional territory, not a province, with
its own Assembly.[^gb-assembly] This record has not researched building
control for other Gilgit-Baltistan towns outside Gilgit city — treat as an
open question.
