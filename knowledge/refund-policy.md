A customer may return an item within 30 days of purchase, provided they have
the receipt. The item must be unused.

Once a return is accepted, the refund reaches the customer within 5 working
days.

## Faulty goods

The 30-day window does not apply to faulty goods. A customer may return a
faulty item outside that window.
